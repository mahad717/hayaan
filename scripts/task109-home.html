Frequently asked questions — ordering, delivery & payment

+−What is Hayaan Market?

Hayaan Market (hayaan.co) is a Somali online marketplace. You can buy electronics, TVs, laptops, phones, power and charging gear, home and office equipment, and health supplements at fixed USD prices, pay securely through EVC Plus, Edahab, and Card, and get your order delivered to your door.

+−Where can I buy electronics near me in Somalia?

Hayaan Market (hayaan.co) is an online electronics store that delivers to your door — every Mogadishu district for $1.50–$3.00 and anywhere else in Somalia for a flat $6.95. Phones, laptops, TVs, printers, routers, air conditioners and home gadgets are all listed at fixed USD prices, and the exact delivery fee shows at checkout before you pay.

+−How do I order from Hayaan Market?

Browse the catalog, add items to your cart, then check out with your name, phone number and delivery district. You pay securely through EVC Plus, Edahab, or Card and the order is delivered to your address. Offices, schools and hotels can also send a bulk request at hayaan.co/quote.

+−How much does delivery cost in Mogadishu?

Delivery inside Mogadishu is priced per district — from $1.50 (Hodan, Waberi, Wadajir) up to $3.00 (Daynile, Kahda, Gubadley, Darussalam, Garasbaaley). The exact fee for your district is shown at checkout before you pay.

+−Is shipping free?

No — delivery is charged on every order: $1.50–$3.00 inside Mogadishu depending on your district, and a flat $6.95 anywhere else in Somalia. The exact fee is shown at checkout before you pay.

+−Do you deliver outside Mogadishu?

Yes. Delivery anywhere else in Somalia is a flat $6.95 regardless of order size. Enter your city at checkout and the fee is calculated automatically before payment.

+−How do I pay?

All orders are paid securely through EVC Plus, Edahab, or Card. Prices are in US dollars and no VAT is charged — the total you confirm at checkout (product price plus the shown delivery fee) is exactly what you pay.

+−Can I buy in bulk for my office, school or shop?

Yes. Use the bulk request page at hayaan.co/quote to describe what you need — printers, laptops, TVs, supplements or a full office setup — and the team will follow up with a tailored offer.

+−How do I contact support?

Message us on WhatsApp at +252 61 599 0144 or email support@hayaan.co. We can help with product questions, order status and delivery details.
